# Odoo Hackathon 2026 Grand Finale

The Odoo Hackathon 2026 Grand Finale is a high-leverage recruitment-focused competition. In India, Odoo uses this hackathon series as their primary platform placement pipeline to screen and hire Software Engineers, offering freshers packages in the ₹5–8 LPA range.

## Event Details
- **Date**: 5–6 September 2026.
- **Location**: Gandhinagar, Gujarat, India (Grand Finale is split across two venues in Gandhinagar, announced post-confirmation).
- **Confirmation Form Deadline**: **9 August 2026** (Failure to submit confirmation removes access).
- **Check-in**: Between 8:00 AM and 9:00 AM on 5 September 2026.
- **Dismissal check**: No departures before 5:30 PM on 6 September 2026.
- **Reimbursement policy**: Odoo does not provide travel or accommodation subsidies.

## Ernakulam (ERS) to Gandhinagar (ADI) Travel Plan

Due to the distance, onward train travel must begin on Wednesday, September 2, to arrive by Friday, September 4.

### 1. Onward Train Journey
- **Train**: `16338 ERS-Okha Express`
- **Departure**: Wednesday, 2 September 2026, 8:25 PM from Ernakulam Junction (`ERS`).
- **Arrival**: Friday, 4 September 2026, 7:00 AM at Ahmedabad Junction (`ADI`).
- **Last-Mile Transfer**: Ahmedabad Metro line connects ADI directly north to Gandhinagar (under 60 minutes).

### 2. Return Train Journey
No direct return trains depart Gandhinagar/Ahmedabad corridors on Sunday night post 5:30 PM.
- **Option A (Monday trains)**:
  - `20924 Humsafar Express` leaves Monday at 3:00 PM, landing back in Ernakulam Tuesday night.
  - `16337 Okha-ERS Express` leaves Monday at 11:20 PM.
- **Option B (Flight)**:
  - Flight back from Ahmedabad Airport (`AMD`) on Sunday night / Monday morning to avoid missing college scheduling constraints.
