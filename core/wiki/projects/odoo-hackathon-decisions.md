# Odoo Hackathon Decision Log

Decision journal and strategy audit trail for the Odoo Hackathon 2026 Grand Finale (Team 583).

## 2026-08-25 — DR-ODOO-01: AssetFlow as Preliminary Entry Point
**Context:** Team needed a strong, demonstrable Odoo module to qualify for the Grand Finale. The preliminary round required a working Odoo application solving a real business problem.
**Decision:** Built AssetFlow — a conflict-prevention physical asset tracker — as the preliminary submission. It leverages PostgreSQL-backed scheduling and an OWL frontend to prevent double-booking of shared equipment.
**Alternatives considered:** Building a simpler CRUD module (rejected to show depth); building outside Odoo ecosystem (invalid per competition rules).
**Status:** active
